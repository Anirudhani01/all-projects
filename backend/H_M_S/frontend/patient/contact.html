Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment online through our booking system, call us at (555) 123-4567, or visit our hospital in person. Online booking is available 24/7.

What insurance do you accept?

We accept most major insurance plans including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, and Medicare. Please contact us to verify your specific insurance coverage.

Do you have emergency services?

Yes, we have a 24/7 emergency department. For life-threatening emergencies, call 911. For urgent but non-life-threatening situations, call our emergency line at (555) 911-HELP.

Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?

Yes, you can cancel or reschedule your appointment up to 24 hours in advance. You can do this online through your patient portal or by calling us.
