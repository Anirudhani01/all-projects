The Product tab contains Product Search feature in openCRX CRM which allows users to efficiently locate specific product information through a detailed, filter-based search form available within the Products section. When users click the dedicated search icon in the Product table, they are presented with a form containing various fields, each tailored for precise filtering using appropriate input types. The form includes Product Name as string field, Product # as numbers fields. Each field can be used individually or in combination, enabling users to quickly narrow down search results based on specific criteria such as availability, dimensions, or version details. This feature is especially valuable for roles like sales representatives, inventory managers, and customer support teams who require quick and accurate access to product data.
