The organization uses openCRX to manage customer relationships and associated products, with an emphasis on maintaining accurate and comprehensive product records. These records are crucial for enabling product visibility and availability in key business processes such as sales orders, quotes, inventory management, and reporting.
The Product Creation module in openCRX empowers administrators and product managers to define and centralize critical product information. This include three form sections called 'General', 'Details', 'System'. General section includes 'Name' field of string data type, 'Description' field of test, 'Product status' as a dropdown menu defaulted to None and contains active or not active, 'Product number' of number, 'Active/Valid from', 'Expires on' both as date type, 'Min positions', 'Max positions', 'Default positions' as numbers, configuration type sub section include 'Min quantity', 'Max quantity', 'Offset quantity', 'Default quantity' as numbers and '[min..max] handling' as a dropdown like [0] N/A to [1] informational, [2] strict enforcement for [min..max] and [3] limit quantity to [min..max] and lastly in General section 'Detailed description' as html or text type. the 'Details' section contains 'Default Price Level', 'Sales tax type', 'Depot assignment group', 'unit of measurement' as dropdowns which has to be selected and the value is written into the field, 'Is stock item' as a checkbox, 'Alternate Product #' as the text field, 'Product usage' as a drop down and number field, 'Allow modification', 'Allow removal' as checkboxes, picture subfield contains 'Title / Source', 'Product Dimension', 'Gross Weight Kilogram', 'Net weight Kilogram' as number fields. System section contains 'identity' as a identifier filed to the product, 'Created at' contains the current data and time, 'Created by', 'Reason' are the sting fields, 'Modified at' will also contains the current time as it is being created, 'Modified by' takes string, 'Disabled' is a checkbox, security sub-section of system contains 'Owning user' as a dropdown as Principal/Name, Principal/Description, Principal/Description(localized) and then string is entered 'Browser access level', 'Update access level', 'Delete access level' as dropdowns having 0-none, 1-private, 2-basic(group members), 3-deep(group members recursively), 4-global(all users). common the another sub section in the system contains 'categories', 'external links' as text fields. "New" button is used to create the new product after filling the necessary details.
